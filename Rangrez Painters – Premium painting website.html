How Your Home Gets Painted In 4 Simple Steps

Experience a completely transparent, organized, and stress-free painting journey from booking to handover.

01

Free Site Visit & Laser Audit

02

100% Masking & Dust-Free Prep

Traditional manual sandpaper sanding generates clouds of hazardous silica dust that enter wardrobes, AC vents, and electronics. Our crew uses industrial vacuum-connected eccentric sanders (HEPA filtration) that capture 99.7% of sanding dust right at the wall surface.

During our free site visit, our technical supervisor uses digital Bosch laser distance meters to measure walls and ceilings precisely. We calculate the exact square footage and deduct large window/door openings so you never pay for unpainted space.
Our written warranty covers paint peeling, bubbling, flaking, and severe shade fading under normal residential use. In the rare event of a defect, our team re-preps and repaints the affected wall completely free of charge.
